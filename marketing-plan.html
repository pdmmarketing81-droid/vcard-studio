5. Kya bolna hai

Dukaan pe pehle 30 second

"Namaste. Ek minute? … Aapka Google pe kitne review hain? [wo batayega, ya phone nikalega]

Dekhiye — jo customer khush hota hai wo chup chala jaata hai. Jo naraz hota hai wahi Google pe likhta hai. Isliye rating girti rehti hai.

Mai counter pe ek QR lagata hoon. Customer star dabata hai. 4–5 star wale seedha Google pe chale jaate hain. 3 ya usse kam wale ek private form pe jaate hain — wo aapke paas aata hai, Google pe nahi.

Saal ka ₹999. Ek naya customer bhi aa gaya to paisa nikal aaya."

Jo sawal aayenge

Wo kahegaTum kaho
"Mehnga hai" "Saal ka ₹999 — mahine ka ₹83. Aapka ek customer kitne ka hai? … to ek customer se hi paisa nikal aaya. Baaki saal muft."
"Mera to Instagram hai" "Instagram pe log tab aate hain jab aapko jaante hain. Google pe wo aate hain jo aapko dhoondh rahe hain. Ye doosri wali baat hai."
"Ye galat nahi hai? Reviews rokna?" "Rokte nahi. Customer chahe to Google pe jaakar likh sakta hai, koi bandish nahi. Hum bas pehle aapse poochte hain — jaise aap khud counter pe poochte ho ki 'sab theek tha na?' Bas wahi, likhit me."
"Baad me batata hoon" "Theek hai. Ek kaam karta hoon — abhi 10 minute me aapka card bana kar link bhejta hoon. Dekh lijiye. Pasand aaye to paisa dijiye."
Yahi sabse zyada chalta hai. Banane me kuch nahi lagta, aur banne ke baad mana karna mushkil hai.
"Mera bhatija banata hai ye sab" "Achhi baat hai. Wo card bana dega. Review wala hissa bhi bana dega? Wo alag cheez hai — star ke hisaab se do alag raste."
